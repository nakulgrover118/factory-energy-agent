Data and preparation 04 / 08

Data is validated first; six traps are handled on purpose

The data pack
meter_15min.csv Energy per 15 minutes for the main supply and 8 sub-meters, over two quarters
tariff.csv Price per kWh by time of day, with a change part-way through
production.csv Daily volume, product mix and shift calendar
weather.csv Daily temperature and degree-days
The traps and how we handle them
Sub-meters do not add up Report an unmetered remainder, never call it waste
Gaps and meter resets Flag gaps; detect resets, never count negative kWh
Mid-quarter tariff change Price every 15-minute interval by its timestamp
Mix shift, no waste Per-product coefficients put it in mix, not waste
Hot period raises cooling Degree-days in the baseline put it in weather
Holiday logged as a shift Use is_holiday and zero units, not the shift calendar
Six validation checks run before any analysis Bad rows are rejected and counted. Loader: CLI script and POST /ingest, same schema.
