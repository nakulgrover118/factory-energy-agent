Approach 05 / 08

Remove the known causes first. What is left is waste.

01 Clean and align

Resample 15-minute meters

Join tariff, production, weather and shifts

Fix gaps, duplicates and resets

02 Baseline

Daily kWh against units per product, degree-days, holidays

Predicts expected energy

Checked with R² and residuals

03 Decompose

Tariff: price change × current kWh

Rest split into volume, mix, weather and waste

Parts add up to the total

04 Find waste

Idle load when output is zero

Night and weekend against daytime load

Leak signature, with evidence

05 Recommend

Savings in kWh, money and CO2e as ranges

Payback and ease of doing

Ranked list; a manager approves

Worked example, in points of last quarter’s bill Toy numbers to show the arithmetic, not data
Last quarter 100.0
Tariff +10.0
Volume +1.5
Mix +1.0
Weather +0.5
Waste +2.0
This quarter, +15% 115.0

Tariff = (new price − old price) × this quarter’s kWh. The energy change is priced at the old price, then split by the baseline into volume, mix, weather and waste. Each part carries a low–high range from a bootstrap.
