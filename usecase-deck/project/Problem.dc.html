The problem 02 / 08

The bill rose 15%. Finance needs to know why before anyone blames a machine.

The situation

The electricity bill is up 15% on last quarter.

The factory meters the main supply and sub-meters on compressors, heating and cooling, furnaces, lighting and production lines.

Finance wants to know whether it was tariff, output, product mix, weather, or energy being wasted.

Why it is hard
1

The causes overlap. Price, output, mix and weather all move at once, and the tariff changed part-way through the period.

2

Blame arrives too early. Old machines look guilty, but waste only counts once price, volume, mix and weather are removed.

3

The data is messy. Gaps, meter resets, sub-meters that do not sum to the main meter, and a holiday logged as a normal shift.

What a good answer looks like
A split of the 15% that adds up
→
Waste found, with evidence
→
Actions with savings ranges and payback
→
Every number traceable to a tool call
